## Contract

`source_bound_pending_inventory_existence_dispute_v1` records the competing
inventories only when the primary is exclusively unlinked `external_compliance`
with `external_duty`, complete typed `unverifiable` human atoms, exact current
quote bindings and distinct IDs. A schema-valid independent no-duty assessment
must have `consistent`, an empty inventory, grounded evidence, and a rationale.

Known machine obligations, registered authoring/verification/ambiguity work,
mixed local/external cues, linked or covered work, malformed/incomplete primary
atoms, unrelated quotes, duplicate/unknown checks and stale source evidence
remain errors. This policy is not a general language classifier; the existing
source compilers recognize a bounded set of facts. Semantic disagreement
outside their knowledge remains pending, not compliant or automatically fixed.

Only explicit `review_draft` may carry this record. Submission and legacy
strict validation still reject it. Both raw response and primary candidate
remain unchanged. No requirement, AO duty or successful verification is created.

## End-to-end retention and release

- The code-owned `source_inventory_disputes` assessment includes the complete
  primary context and independent result plus current source/check hashes.
- The AO ledger retains assessments separately from inventoried AO duties,
  under its existing run/case/chunk/candidate/request/response bindings.
- The bridge envelope must be `completed_with_disputes`,
  `coverage_complete=false`, `submission_ready=false`.
- Both bridge and pipeline consumers reconstruct the assessment from the
  canonical current request and raw-response compilation. Merely rehashing a
  modified receipt or ledger does not make a changed/deleted assessment valid.
- The pipeline creates a human task to decide **whether the duty exists**,
  displays both opinions, and explicitly says not to execute a guessed duty.
  Its assessment hash participates in MO identity and serialized MR markers.
  Full producer evidence remains available without inventing an AO mapping.
- Draft scoring retains the pending human task; it is not verified coverage or
  submission authority. A high aggregate score cannot remove the release gate.
